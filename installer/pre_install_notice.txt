Tachyon Transcripts — Important Legal Notice
=============================================

Please read the following before installing.

Tachyon Transcripts is a recording and transcription tool.  Recording
other people without their knowledge or consent may be illegal under
the laws of your jurisdiction.

IT IS YOUR RESPONSIBILITY to understand and comply with the laws
that apply to you.  The developer makes no legal guarantees and is
not liable for how you use this software.

Key points to consider:

  *  United States -- federal law is a "one-party consent" rule
     (the recorder counts as a party), but eleven states require
     all-party consent: California, Connecticut, Florida, Illinois,
     Maryland, Massachusetts, Michigan, Montana, Nevada, New
     Hampshire, Pennsylvania, and Washington.  In these states you
     generally must have every participant's consent before
     recording.

  *  European Union / UK -- the General Data Protection Regulation
     (GDPR) and the UK Data Protection Act apply to recordings
     containing identifiable voices.  You typically need a lawful
     basis (usually consent) and must inform participants.

  *  Workplace meetings -- your employer's policies, employment
     contract, and applicable labour law may prohibit recording
     meetings.  Recording unionised workplaces may trigger
     additional protections.

  *  Meeting platforms -- Zoom, Microsoft Teams, Google Meet, Slack
     Huddles and similar services include their own recording
     notice rules in their Terms of Service.  Using Tachyon
     Transcripts to bypass those notices may violate the ToS even
     when local law would otherwise permit recording.

  *  Confidential / privileged conversations -- attorney-client,
     doctor-patient, clergy, and similar relationships are almost
     always governed by stricter rules than general conversations.

If you are unsure whether you may legally record a particular
conversation, do not record it.

When you first launch the application you will be asked to confirm
that you have read and understood these notes.  Recording is
disabled until you do so.
